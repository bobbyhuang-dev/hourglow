HourGlow is a macOS menu bar wallpaper scheduler that follows the daylight. Choose system aerials or local images and switch them at a fixed time, relative to sunrise or sunset, or throughout a solar phase.

## Start here

| Guide | What you will learn |
| --- | --- |
| [Getting started](https://github.com/bobbyhuang-dev/hourglow/wiki/Getting-Started) | Install the app, find the menu bar panel, set your location, and enable launch at login. |
| [Scheduling and location](https://github.com/bobbyhuang-dev/hourglow/wiki/Scheduling-and-Location) | Choose triggers, save slot edits, and understand manual wallpaper changes. |
| [Importing wallpaper sets](https://github.com/bobbyhuang-dev/hourglow/wiki/Importing-Wallpaper-Sets) | Turn a folder of images or a `.sundialScene` into a full-day timeline. |
| [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/bobbyhuang-dev/hourglow/wiki/Troubleshooting) | Resolve common problems and gather useful diagnostics. |
